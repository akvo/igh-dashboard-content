Products are tracked where they prevent or treat the condition or its symptoms. For endometriosis that includes pain, migration of endometrial tissue, endometrial lesions and related infertility, and extends to products regulating the menstrual cycle. For polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome it includes products targeting hyperandrogenism, metabolic regulation, fertility and menstrual cycle regulation. For uterine fibroids it includes menstrual cycle regulation, shrinking or preventing fibroids, and pain management.
