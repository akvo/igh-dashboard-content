Maternal enteric microbiome covers interventions directed at the maternal enteric microbiome and at environmental enteric dysfunction. Two kinds qualify: foods enriched to deliver a physiological benefit the unmodified food does not, such as polyphenols, microbial metabolites, glycans, bioactive peptides and fermented foods; and live biotherapeutic products intended to colonise the gut, such as faecal microbiota transplantation. Not tracked: interventions directed at a microbiome other than the enteric one, including vaginal microbiome therapies; and interventions with no standardised dosing or formulation.
