Maternal anaemia is limited to iron deficiency-related anaemia. Products qualify where they prevent or treat it, directly stimulate erythropoiesis, or supply nutrients that erythroid precursor cells need. Both oral and intravenous iron supplementation are recorded as drugs, for consistency. Not tracked: anaemia arising from other micronutrient deficiencies such as folate or vitamin B12; conventional foods; supplements or traditional medicines that are not properly dosed or formulated for use.
