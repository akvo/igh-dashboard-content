**Snakebite envenoming** — drugs only where developed for low- and middle-income country needs, such as antivenoms incorporating small-molecule inhibitors or heat-stable venom-agnostic oral drugs, or supporting registration there. Biologics only where they are antivenom immunoglobulins based on the venom of snakes from those countries, or support registration there. Diagnostics only where capable of identifying envenomation by medically important snake species common to those countries.
