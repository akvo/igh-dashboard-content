Targeted sources are selected for particular diseases, product areas or stages of development, rather than searched across every area in every cycle. They include product development portfolios published by organisations working in each disease area, and research grant databases maintained by major funders of global health R&D. They are sources of new information in their own right: candidates enter the pipeline from them directly, and grant databases in particular are a primary route to discovery and preclinical work that has not reached a trial registry. The difference between common and targeted sources is one of coverage, not standing.
