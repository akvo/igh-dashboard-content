Chemical vector control products are limited to active ingredients and formulations intended for public health use that inhibit, kill or repel vectors: new insecticides and formulations for treated nets and indoor residual spraying, systemic insecticides and endectocides, insecticide-based baits and traps, spatial repellents, and chemical larvicides. Biological vector control products are limited to innovative biological interventions such as microbial larvicides, sterilisation techniques and genetic modification. Reservoir-targeted vaccines are limited to veterinary vaccines developed specifically to prevent animal-to-human transmission.
