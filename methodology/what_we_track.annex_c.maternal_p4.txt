Devices for postpartum haemorrhage must have standalone action to prevent or control bleeding: balloon, suction or sponge tamponades, tools assisting bimanual compression, and devices with wider application that have demonstrated use against postpartum haemorrhage. A device combined with a pharmaceutical element qualifies only where the device and the pharmaceutical element each act independently against the bleed. Not tracked: devices used in management that do not control bleeding, such as cell salvage for blood replacement; diagnostic devices such as those measuring blood loss; devices that only deliver a medicine, which are captured as medicines instead; digital tools guiding diagnosis and management; and embolisation and surgical procedures.
