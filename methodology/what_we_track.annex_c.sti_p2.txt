Drugs and biologics are tracked where they prevent transmission, prevent acquisition of the infection, or clear it. Vaccines are tracked on the same basis, whether preventive or therapeutic. Microbicides are tracked where they are topical products preventing transmission of one of the three infections and are suitable for low- and middle-income settings — heat stable and easy to use, for instance. Diagnostics cover both tests for the infection itself and tests for antimicrobial resistance.
