Drugs and biologics are tracked where they prevent transmission, prevent acquisition of the infection, or clear it. Vaccines are tracked on the same basis, whether preventive or therapeutic, though a vaccine developed for a therapeutic indication is recorded as a biologic. Microbicides are tracked where they are topical products preventing transmission of one of the three infections and are suitable for low- and middle-income settings — heat stable and easy to use, for instance. Diagnostics cover both tests for the infection itself and tests for antimicrobial resistance.
