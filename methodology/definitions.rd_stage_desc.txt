Product areas do not all move through development in the same way, so the pipeline does not force every record onto a single stage scale. Drugs, vaccines, biologics and microbicides are staged from discovery and preclinical work through Phase I, II and III to approved. Diagnostics move from early development to late development to approved. Chemical vector control products move from screening and optimisation through development to prequalification listing or regulatory approval. Biological vector control products use their own Phase I to III scale, so a Phase II biological vector control product is not equivalent to a Phase II drug. Devices in clinical development with no clinical phase listed in the registry are marked as human safety and efficacy. Where there is no usable stage information, the record is shown as unclear or not applicable rather than assigned a stage.
