Product families do not develop along the same path, so the pipeline uses several stage scales rather than forcing them into one. Drugs, vaccines, biologics and microbicides run from discovery and preclinical work through Phases I, II, III and IV to approved. Diagnostics run from early development to late development to approved. Chemical vector control products run from primary and secondary screening and optimisation, through development, to prequalification listing and regulatory approval. Biological vector control products use Phase I to IV labels on a scale of their own; a Phase II there is not the milestone a Phase II drug has reached. A record with no usable stage is shown as unclear or not applicable rather than assigned one.
