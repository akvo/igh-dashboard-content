An at-a-glance view of health products approved or in development across three global health areas: neglected diseases, emerging infectious diseases and women’s health.

**Home** — a snapshot of research activity, where trials and developers are located, how the pipeline is spread across development stages, and how progress has shifted over time.

**Pipeline Overview** — filter and explore the data by global health area, disease, product type and R&D stage.

**Pipeline Explorer** — explore candidates, approved products, clinical trials and technology types, as visuals or as a custom table.

**Pipeline Trends** — see how the pipeline evolves over time and compare portfolios.

**WHO Priority alignment** — map the pipeline against the World Health Organization’s product-development priorities.
